Proof

At toy scale every value is v plus at most 16 key bits, so at most 17 octets. Every leaf value is therefore embedded, and the hash-only form (for a leaf in the proof whose value was not asked for and is longer than 32 octets) never appears here, nor does the long form. Hashes are labels, not real blake2b output, and key suffixes are W − d bits instead of 248 − d.
