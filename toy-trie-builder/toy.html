Proof

At toy scale the key width W stands in for the JIP's 248 bits: raw key suffixes are W − d bits and a key takes ⌈W/8⌉ octets. Every value is v plus at most 16 key bits, so at most 17 octets, and every raw value therefore uses an embedded form octet (its length); form 33, the 32-octet hash of a value longer than 32 octets, is implemented but never produced here. Hash labels such as h(N10) name toy 32-octet hashes, not blake2b output; the labels do not show the cleared bit 7 of a left child's hash, which is irrelevant at toy scale, but the toy hex does clear it.
